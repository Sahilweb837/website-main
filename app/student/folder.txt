--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROUTE 1: /student/login
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Dedicated student authentication portal.
- Target File: app/student/login/page.tsx
- Features:
  * Student Email & Password / Mobile OTP authentication.
  * Google One-Tap & Firebase SSO support.
  * Instant "1-Click Demo Student Access" (Aarav Patel - Full Stack Cohort).
  * Direct session initialization and redirection to /student/dashboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROUTE 2: /student/dashboard
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Primary student workspace and progress cockpit.
- Target File: app/student/dashboard/page.tsx
- Features:
  * Student Welcome Header with enrollment ID & cohort batch.
  * Live DRM Watermark Security status indicator.
  * 4 Core Metrics: Active Courses, Overall Progress, Attendance Rate, Fee Status.
  * "Continue Learning" action card directly launching /lms/classroom.
  * Enrolled Courses progress list.
  * Live class schedules and mentor notifications.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROUTE 3: /student/courses
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Student course management and learning track curriculum.
- Target File: app/student/courses/page.tsx
- Features:
  * Enrolled Courses and Catalog explorer tabs.
  * Filter by category and search keyword.
  * Progress percentage and completed lecture counter.
  * Launch video player / classroom with single click.
  * Syllabi preview with interactive module accordion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROUTE 4: /student/payment
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Fee management, payment history, and instant course checkout.
- Target File: app/student/payment/page.tsx
- Features:
  * Fee summary cards: Total Fees, Paid, Balance Due, Next Due Date.
  * Interactive Online Fee Payment modal (UPI, Cards, Netbanking).
  * Invoices & Payment Ledger with Printable/Downloadable Receipt modal.
  * Course checkout integration via ?course=<slug>.
