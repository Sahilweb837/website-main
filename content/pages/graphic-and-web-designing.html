Professional Graphic & Web Designing Training

Course outline

This is our course outline you can check below

  • 1.

    Introduction to Design Principles

    Discover the fundamentals of design by investigating components such as line, shape, color, texture, and space. Recognize fundamental concepts like unity, rhythm, balance, contrast, and emphasis. Utilize these design principles in a variety of settings to produce visually stunning and useful designs.

  • 2.

    Basics of Digital Tools and Software

    Start by learning how to edit basic images in Adobe Photoshop and creating vector graphics in Adobe Illustrator. Discover the foundations of UI/UX design with programs like Sketch or Figma, and master layout design with Adobe InDesign. These abilities serve as the cornerstone of expert digital design.

  • 3.

    Introduction to Web Design

    Discover the fundamentals of web design by beginning with an overview of HTML for elements and structure and a basic understanding of CSS for layout and styling. To create websites that look amazing on all devices, understand the fundamentals of responsive design.

  • 4.

    Introduction to Branding and Visual Communication

    Discover the foundations of visual communication and logo design, as well as branding and identity design. Recognize the significance of typography in developing identity systems and brand assets that flow together. Discover how to create fundamental brand components that clearly convey a company's mission and core values.

  • 5.

    Advanced Graphic Design Principles

    Dive into complex color theory and its applications, as well as advanced design principles and elements. To improve your designs, experiment with expressive typography and other advanced typographic techniques. Develop the ability to modify your designs for a range of platforms and media to ensure their adaptability and efficacy in diverse settings.

  • 6.

    Advanced Web Design

    Develop your web design abilities by learning sophisticated HTML/CSS techniques. Pay particular attention to media queries a Focus on responsive design with media queries and flexible layouts to improve your web design abilities. Learn advanced HTML/CSS techniques. Develop strong, dynamic websites by learning the fundamentals of content management systems (CMS) and back-end development, as well as front-end frameworks like Foundation and Bootstrap.

  • 7.

    Advanced UI/UX Design

    Gain expertise in user experience (UX) design by delving deeply into complex subjects. Develop your ability to perform user research and usability testing efficiently to help with design decisions. To develop digital products that are focused on the needs of users, apply design thinking techniques and iterative design processes. Make sure your designs satisfy the varied needs and preferences of your users by putting a strong emphasis on accessibility and inclusivity.

  • 8.

    Integration and Specialization

    To produce unified and successful design solutions, combine the principles of web and graphic design. Select a specialty that reflects your interests, such as social media design or motion graphics, and create extensive projects that highlight your abilities. Develop and broaden your design portfolio via practical experience and group projects to get ready for a lucrative career in the creative sector.

  • 9.

    Industry Immersion and Final Project

    Attend professional workshops and guest lectures to get a deep understanding of the industry. Work together with industry partners on practical projects that will culminate in a presentation of the capstone project. To bolster your knowledge and prepare for the design industry, consider career options in graphic, web, and user experience design.
