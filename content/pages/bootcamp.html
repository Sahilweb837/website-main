Career Roadmap

Your 6-Month Career Transformation Journey

Go from beginner to industry-ready professional through structured learning, real-world projects, resume building, interview preparation, internship experience, and placement support.

01
Month 01

Career Foundation & Professional Growth

Start your journey with career planning, industry orientation, communication skills, Git & GitHub, LinkedIn profile setup, and professional mindset development. Build a strong foundation for long-term success in the tech industry.

02
Month 02

Skill Development & Portfolio Projects

Learn industry-relevant technologies and begin creating practical projects. Understand modern development workflows, responsive design principles, and collaborative project practices used in real companies.

03
Month 03

Advanced Development & Real-World Applications

Build complete applications using modern tools and frameworks. Work with databases, APIs, authentication systems, deployment platforms, and real-world business requirements to strengthen your technical expertise.

04
Month 04

Resume Building & Interview Preparation

Create an ATS-friendly resume, optimize your LinkedIn profile, build a professional portfolio website, and prepare for HR and technical interviews through expert-led mock interview sessions.

05
Month 05

Live Projects & Internship Experience

Work on industry-level projects, collaborate with mentors, gain hands-on internship experience, understand professional workflows, and build confidence by solving real business challenges.

06
Month 06

Placement Support & Career Launch

Participate in placement-focused training, advanced mock interviews, aptitude preparation, company-specific interview rounds, and career guidance. Graduate with a strong portfolio, internship experience, and job-ready skills.
