Course outline

This is our professional 2D & 3D Animation Course at Netcoder Technology, Dharamshala designed for beginners and aspiring professionals.

  • 1.

    Introduction to Animation

    Learn the history and principles of animation, including the 12 principles of motion. Explore animation techniques such as 2D, 3D, stop-motion, and motion graphics. Get an overview of the animation pipeline followed in leading animation training institutes worldwide.

  • 2.

    Sketching and Character Design

    Master the fundamentals of sketching, perspective, proportions, and anatomy. Learn character design and animation sketching used in professional studios. This module helps you build a strong base for careers in animation and VFX.

  • 3.

    2D Animation Fundamentals

    Understand the basics of 2D animation with tools like Adobe Animate and Toon Boom Harmony. Practice lip-syncing, keyframe animation, and storytelling to bring characters to life. Perfect for those looking for animation classes in Dharamshala.

  • 4.

    Motion Graphics & Compositing

    Create engaging explainer videos, logo animations, and motion graphics using Adobe After Effects. Learn compositing techniques to merge elements seamlessly – a must-have skill for students exploring VFX courses in Himachal Pradesh.

  • 5.

    3D Modeling & Texturing

    Gain hands-on training in Autodesk Maya and Blender. Learn polygon modeling, UV mapping, and texturing for creating detailed 3D characters and environments. Includes basics of sculpting for those interested in 3D design courses.

  • 6.

    Rigging & 3D Animation

    Develop skills in rigging, skinning, and facial rigging. Animate walk cycles, body mechanics, and advanced character movements. This section builds strong foundations for a career in 3D animation and character animation courses.

  • 7.

    Lighting, Rendering & VFX

  • 8.

    Video Editing for Animators

    Students are introduced to professional editing software such as Adobe Premiere Pro. The focus will be on sequencing animated shots, adding transitions and titles, and preparing the final cut for delivery.

  • 9.

    Sound Design and Audio Syncing

    This module teaches the fundamentals of sound design, including background music, voiceovers, and sound effects. Students will learn how to sync dialogues with lip movement and enhance the storytelling impact of their animations.

  • 10.

    Final Project & Portfolio Development

    Work on a capstone project such as a short animated film. Build a professional demo reel showcasing 2D and 3D animation, modeling, and compositing. Netcoder Technology also guides you with animation portfolio development and career placement support.
