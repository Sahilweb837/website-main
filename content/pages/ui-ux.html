Course outline

This is our course outline you can check below

  • 1.

    Introduction To UX

    UX (User Experience) is the holistic quality of interaction that users have with a product or service, encompassing their perceptions, emotions, and behaviors throughout the entire experience.

  • 2.

    UX Law

    UX laws are principles and guidelines derived from user experience research and psychology that inform the design of products and services to enhance usability, satisfaction, and overall user experience.

  • 3.

    UX Research

    UX research is the systematic study of users' behaviors, needs, and preferences to inform the design and improvement of products or services, aiming to enhance usability and user satisfaction.

  • 4.

    Introduction To UI

    UI typically refers to an initial overview or basic understanding of User Interface design principles and concepts, emphasizing elements such as layout, visual design, and user interaction.

  • 5.

    Building Blocks Of Visual Design

    The bUIlding blocks of visual design are the fundamental elements used to create visual compositions. These typically include elements like color, typography, shape, line, texture, and space. Each of these components contributes to the overall aesthetic and communication of a design.

  • 6.

    Typography

    Typography refers to the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and visually appealing. It involves selecting typefaces, adjusting font sizes, spacing, and alignment to effectively communicate the intended message in a design or piece of text.

  • 7.

    Color Theory

    Color theory is the study of how colors interact with each other and how they are perceived by viewers. It explores principles such as color harmony, contrast, and the psychological effects of different colors.

  • 8.

    Logo Design

    Logo design is the process of creating a unique visual symbol or mark that represents a brand, company, or organization. It involves combining typography, imagery, color, and other design elements to communicate the essence and values of the entity it represents.

  • 9.

    Iconography

    Iconography is the study or use of symbols and icons to convey meaning, often in the context of visual communication, art, or design.

  • 10.

    Moodboard

    A moodboard is a visual collage or collection of images, text, and other elements that represent the desired mood, style, or aesthetic for a project.

  • 11.

    Designing For Mobile

    Designing for mobile involves creating digital interfaces and experiences specifically tailored to smartphones and tablets. It reqUIres consideration of factors such as small screen sizes, touch-based interaction, and varied device capabilities. Mobile design focuses on optimizing user interfaces for efficient navigation, readability, and usability on mobile devices.

  • 12.

    Grid System

    A grid system in UI/UX design is a structured layout framework consisting of vertical and horizontal lines used to organize content and elements on a screen. It helps designers achieve consistency, balance, and alignment in their designs, making it easier for users to navigate and understand the interface.

  • 13.

    Starting With Design

    "Starting with design" in UI/UX involves the initial stages of creating digital products or experiences, where designers begin by defining goals, understanding user needs, and conceptualizing design concepts.

  • 14.

    Android & iOS guidelines

    Android and iOS gUIdelines are sets of design principles, standards, and best practices provided by Google and Apple respectively for developers and designers creating apps for their respective platforms.

  • 15.

    Latest Design Trends

    The latest design trends in UI/UX encompass emerging approaches, styles, and techniques that shape the visual and experiential aspects of digital products. These trends often include elements such as minimalism, neumorphism, dark mode, immersive experiences, micro-interactions, and personalized user interfaces, among others.

What Is The Difference Between UI & UX Design?

UI Design focuses on the visual appearance of digital products, while UX Design focuses on improving user experience, usability, and interaction flow.

Why Is UI/UX Design Important For Websites & Apps?

UI/UX Design helps businesses create visually attractive, user-friendly, and engaging digital experiences that improve customer satisfaction and interaction.

Will I Learn Figma & Practical Design Tools During Training?
